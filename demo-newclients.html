How long will it take to see results?

Results from skin treatments depend on many factors: your skin condition, goals, lifestyle habits, and your consistency with homecare.

  • Visible improvements can begin within a couple of treatments
  • Transformational results are often achieved over 6-12 months with consistency

For long-term maintenance, we recommend visiting every 4-6 weeks, as skin cells take about 28 days to turn over. Consistency is key!

We also offer affordable weekly or fortnightly payment plan options to suit your budget.

Contraindications

For your safety and to ensure the best possible results, certain skin and health conditions may limit which treatments are available. Please let us know if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Are currently taking Roaccutane such as Isotretinoin/Accutane
  • Are taking blood-thinning medication
  • Have recently undergone cosmetic surgery or anti-wrinkle treatments
  • Have active skin conditions such as eczema or psoriasis in the treatment area

If you're unsure whether a treatment is suitable for you, your therapist will guide you at your first appointment.
