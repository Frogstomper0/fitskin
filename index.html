SIGNATURE

Fit Skin Facial

Our signature Pelactiv facial combines deep cleansing, exfoliation and hydration with LED light therapy to promote skin rejuvenation.
  • Ideal first step if you’re new to the clinic or unsure where to start
  • Includes consult, cleanse, exfoliation and extractions as needed

  • Can be layered with add-ons like LED or gentle peels for extra impact

THE FIT SKIN CLINIC WAY

How we work with your skin

From first consult to in-clinic treatments, salon finishes and gifting — everything connects into one plan, not one-off appointments.

  • A single therapist curating your plan from first consult to maintenance.
  • Treatment blocks designed in phases – calm, correct, then maintain.
  • Adjustments at each visit based on how your skin is actually behaving.
  • Streamlined homecare that works together (and plays nicely with actives).
  • Long-term plan for birthdays, events and skin milestones, not just one-offs.
