Good to know

  • Individually tailored settings based on your skin type and goals.
  • Most treatments feel warm with a light prickly sensation.
  • Typical sessions take 30–45 minutes depending on the option chosen.
  • Best results usually seen as a short series rather than a one‑off.

Treatment menu

  • Avance Fractional Laser – focuses on texture, fine lines and mild scarring while supporting collagen over time.
  • Brightening Laser Toning – helps to even overall tone and target diffuse pigment and dullness.
  • Luxe Carbon Laser Facial – combines a carbon mask with laser to deeply cleanse, smooth and decongest.
  • Express Carbon Laser – a shortened option for maintenance or before an event.

Your therapist will advise which option is most suitable after a skin assessment and discussion of your goals.

FAQs

Is there downtime?

Most people leave slightly pink and feel a mild warmth for a few hours. You may experience temporary dryness or flakiness over the following days. We’ll run through aftercare to keep your skin comfortable.

How many sessions will I need?

You may notice a fresher look after one visit, but tone and texture concerns generally respond best to a series of 3–6 sessions, spaced several weeks apart.

Who is not suitable?

Certain medications, recent sun exposure, pregnancy and some medical conditions can make laser unsuitable. We’ll check this carefully during your consultation and may suggest alternative treatments if needed.
