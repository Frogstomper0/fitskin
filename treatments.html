Common Questions

We treat acne, pigmentation, fine lines, sun damage, uneven skin tone, and unwanted hair. Services include advanced facials, laser & IPL, skin needling, chemical peels and more.

Many clients notice improvement after a single session. Concerns like pigmentation or hair removal typically need a course of 4-6 treatments. We'll map out a personalised plan at your skin consultation.

Most treatments involve minimal downtime - mild redness or warmth for a few hours. Skin needling may cause redness for 24-48 hours. Full aftercare instructions are provided before you leave.
