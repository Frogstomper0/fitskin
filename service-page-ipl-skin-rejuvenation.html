IPL Skin Rejuvenation (Photofirm)

Gentle bursts of light to help even tone, support collagen and refresh photo-damaged skin.

Book now See details

How it works

Photofirm IPL delivers controlled pulses of light that are absorbed by pigment and vascular targets in the skin. Heat is converted in a way that can soften the look of redness and sun spots while encouraging fresh collagen over time.

Benefits

  • Helps diffuse redness and mild flushing on the face.
  • Targets scattered sun damage and early signs of ageing.
  • Minimal downtime when homecare and sun protection are followed.

Good for / Not for

  • Good for: mild redness, early photo-ageing and patchy complexion in fair to medium skin tones.
  • Not for: recently tanned skin, photosensitising medications, pregnancy or certain medical conditions.

What to expect

  • Appointment time: Approx. 30–45 minutes depending on area (face / face & neck / face, neck & décolletage).
  • Sensation: Warm flicks or snaps with brief heat; settings are adjusted to your comfort level.
  • Downtime: Temporary redness and mild warmth; sun spots may darken before gently flaking away.

Aftercare

  • Avoid hot showers, intense exercise and saunas for 24 hours after treatment.
  • Use gentle, non-exfoliating skincare and strict SPF 30+ while your course is in progress.

We will always complete a skin assessment and test patch before a full course to make sure Photofirm is suitable for you.

Pricing

  • Single session — From $XX (area-dependent)
  • Best booked as a course of 3–6 treatments for cumulative benefits.
  • Packages for combined face, neck and décolletage can be tailored to your budget and goals.

Add-ons / Pair with

  • LED Light Therapy to calm the skin and support recovery.
  • Professional facials or peels between sessions to maintain overall skin health.
