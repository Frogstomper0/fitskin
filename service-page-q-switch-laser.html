Q-Switch Laser & Tattoo Removal

Precise laser pulses aimed at stubborn pigment and selected tattoos, staged safely over time.

Book now See details

How it works

Q-switch laser uses short, targeted pulses of energy to break pigment or ink into tiny fragments that the body can gradually process. Treatments are always preceded by a detailed consultation and, where needed, patch testing to check how your skin responds.

Benefits

  • Targets isolated sun spots and age spots on face or body.
  • Can soften or remove selected cosmetic tattoos where appropriate.
  • Staged, controlled approach designed around your goals and skin type.

Good for / Not for

  • Good for: small to medium areas of unwanted pigment or suitable cosmetic tattoo ink.
  • Not for: heavily tanned skin, unstable medical conditions, pregnancy or unrealistic expectations for complete removal in one visit.

What to expect

  • Appointment time: Approx. 15–45 minutes depending on the size and number of areas.
  • Sensation: Sharp elastic-band snaps with warmth in the treated area.
  • Downtime: Temporary whitening, redness or pinpoint crusting that settles over several days with correct aftercare.

Aftercare

  • Keep the area clean, dry and protected; avoid picking at any flaking or crusting.
  • Strictly avoid sun exposure on the treated area and use SPF 30+ daily.

Not all pigment or tattoos can be safely treated. We’ll confirm suitability and outline a realistic treatment plan during your consultation.

Pricing

  • Single session — From $XX (area-dependent)
  • Multi-session packages available and recommended for most tattoo and pigment work.
  • Final pricing is based on the size, colour and complexity of the area treated.

Add-ons / Pair with

  • LED Light Therapy to support healing in the days after treatment.
  • Top-up laser sessions at agreed intervals to build on results gradually.
